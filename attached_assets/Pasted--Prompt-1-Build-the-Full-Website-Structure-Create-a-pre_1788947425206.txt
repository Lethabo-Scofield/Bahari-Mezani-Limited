## Prompt 1 — Build the Full Website Structure

Create a premium, modern 6-page marketing website for **Bahari Mezani Limited**, a Kenyan blue-economy company based in Kilifi, coastal Kenya.

The company operates **Samaki Freshi**, a solar-powered cold-chain system designed to reduce post-harvest fish loss by placing off-grid cold storage directly at fish landing sites and transporting fish through refrigerated logistics.

This is a marketing and company website, not a web application.

### Pages

Create:

1. Home — `/`
2. About Us — `/about`
3. Cold Storage & Fleet — `/cold-storage`
4. Products — `/products`
5. Impact — `/impact`
6. Contact — `/contact`

### Brand Direction

The visual identity should feel:

* Serious and commercially credible
* Premium but accessible
* Coastal East African
* Infrastructure-focused
* Human and community-driven
* Suitable for B2B buyers, investors, development partners and consumers

Do not make it look like a generic NGO or sustainability template.

Avoid:

* Beige sustainability aesthetics
* Terracotta palettes
* Excessive gradients
* Excessive rounded cards
* Decorative icons everywhere
* Generic stock-business imagery
* Over-animation

### Colours

Use:

* Navy: `#0D2B45`
* Teal: `#00796B`
* Light teal: `#E0F5F2`
* Gold: `#C8A84B`
* White: `#FFFFFF`
* Off-white: `#F7F9F8`
* Primary text: `#1A1A2E`
* Secondary text: `#5A6474`
* Border: `#DDE5E3`

### Typography

Headlines:
**DM Serif Display**

Body/UI:
**Inter**

### Global Design

Use:

* 8px spacing system
* Generous section padding
* Maximum body text width around 68 characters
* Cards with approximately 12px radius
* Buttons around 6px radius
* Very subtle shadows only
* Strong photography
* Large statistics
* Large editorial headlines
* Responsive layouts designed properly for 1440px, 768px and 390px

Use subtle fades and scroll reveals where appropriate, but avoid unnecessary animation.
